1 · Sign inClerk verifies Google or email credentials and issues a session.
2 · RedirectBack to the page that was requested, else Overview. Loading shows a neutral shell, never stale data.
3 · API callsConsole sends the Clerk session token to FastAPI, which verifies it and checks workspace membership.
4 · No accessSigned in but not a member: the 403 state, with sign out. No data is requested.
5 · Sign outClears the session and returns to Sign in; open charts are discarded.
After sign-in the console opens on Overview with the data-source scope restored from the URL. Preview data stays labelled until real agents are connected. Not built or claimed: the Clerk integration, workspace membership checks and the token verification endpoint do not exist yet. This board is the target flow.
